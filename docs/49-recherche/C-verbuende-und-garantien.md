## 0. The short version for modelling

1. **Almost every Verbund tariff sets the same numbers as DT Teil A/C for rail delays.**
   - Single tickets: 25 % at 60–119 min and 50 % from 120 min, of the price actually paid.
   - Time tickets: a flat 1,50 € per case in 2nd class and 2,25 € in 1st class.
   - Time-ticket cap: 25 % of the price actually paid.
   - Payout threshold: 4,00 €.
   - These rules cover the rail leg only. Bus, tram and U-Bahn never count.
2. **The differences between Verbünde are real and matter for the model:**
   - **Does a day ticket count as a time ticket?** In most tariffs it does: VBB, RMV, HVV, MVV, SH-Tarif, VBN, MDV and NRW ("Gültigkeit von einem Tag oder länger").
   - **Is there a minimum number of cases?** NRW (VRR, VRS, AVV, WestfalenTarif, NRW-Tarif) requires "wiederholt (mindestens 3)" cases, so at least 3 × 1,50 = 4,50 €.
   - **When does a time-ticket case count?**
     - Niedersachsentarif counts a time-ticket case from **20 min**: "wiederholt mit einer Verspätung von mindestens 20 Minuten", 1,50 € per case.
     - RMV's own FAQ lets you pool delays under 60 min at the same EVU.
     - DT Teil C pools delays of 20 min or more; every full 60 min is one case.
     - All the others need 60 min per case.
   - **Deadlines differ.** The usual deadline is 1 year after the ticket expires. **MVV allows only 3 months.** Single tickets pay out once per ticket.
   - **Special caps:**
     - HVV: Semesterticket at most 4,50 € per semester.
     - Bicycle day ticket: 0,40 € per trip (VBB, HVV, MVV, VBN, MDV standard).
     - SH-Tarif: time tickets paid by a Schulkostenträger can be claimed only by that Schulkostenträger.
     - The common standard: free carriage means a claim base of 0 €.
3. **The claim goes to the railway (EVU) whose train was late, never to the Verbund.** Most tariffs point to the Servicecenter Fahrgastrechte (60647 Frankfurt). The exceptions:
   - RMV runs its own central office for RMV tariff tickets.
   - Some EVUs sit outside the SC FGR: NordWestBahn/Transdev, WestfalenBahn, and BRB (via Transdev).
4. **Assignment to a third party (the NGO) is allowed in the common SPNV standard text.** It names as claimant "Derjenige, an den der Fahrgast seinen Anspruch abgetreten hat", and the carrier "kann für die Abtretung einen Nachweis verlangen".
5. **Voluntary guarantees start at 5–30 min and include bus and tram**, but each has its own conditions:
   - Most exclude Deutschland-Tickets bought outside the region, and some exclude all D-Tickets.
   - Deadlines are short: 3–14 days.
   - Most pay to the ticket holder personally. NVV pays cash only, at a counter.
   - NRW, VRN and VVS pay only against a taxi receipt: nothing is paid unless the passenger actually took a taxi.
   - **Fixed-money guarantees that can be modelled:**
     - MVG München: 5 € when over 20 min.
     - S-Bahn München: 4 € when over 30 min.
     - ÜSTRA/GVH Hannover: 5 € for a time ticket when over 20 min.
     - KVV: 2,50 € when over 30 min.
     - NVV: more than 5 min, 1 € per trip for the D-Ticket.
     - BRB: from 15 min, 1,00 € for a time ticket.
     - VVO: from 15 min, but the benefit is a ticket, not money.
   - **Ended:** RMV 10-Minuten-Garantie (1 Feb 2024), hvv Garantie (1 May 2023), NAH.SH-Garantie (30 Nov 2024).

### 1a. The common standard text: „Fahrgastrechte im Schienenpersonennahverkehr bei Zugverspätungen, Zugausfällen und resultierenden Anschlussversäumnissen"

VBB §14, RMV GBB §15, HVV, MVV §17 and VBN reproduce its core sentences almost word for word. Its key rules:

## 5. Open questions

1. **AVV, WestfalenTarif and NRW-Tarif**: confirm the 2026 "BB für die Verbund- und Gemeinschaftstarife in NRW" still says „mindestens 3" and names day tickets. The last file found on westfalentarif.de is dated 2024-01-01; VRR and VRS 2026 confirm it.
2. **Tariffs that defer to the EVU's BB** (bwtarif, KVV, VGN, VRM, saarVV, VMS, ÜSTRA): which BB actually applies to a Verbund time ticket on a DB Regio, Arverio, go-ahead or agilis train? DB's BB contain no Nahverkehr 1,50 € rule, and DT C covers only DT tickets. In practice the SC FGR pays 1,50 € (bwegt, ÜSTRA pages), but I found no binding text. The EBA says the Länder supervise.
3. **Cap basis for an Abo paid monthly** outside VRR: is it the monthly instalment or the annual price? The texts say „tatsächlich gezahlter Zeitkartenpreis". Ask the SC FGR, or read a rejection letter.
4. **MVV's 3-month deadline** is shorter than the DT/EU one year. Is it enforceable against Art. 19 VO 2021/782 plus CIV Art. 60? Model 3 months to be safe for MVV tickets.
5. **VRN's exclusion of the D-Ticket** from rail compensation conflicts with DT C 8. Treat the D-Ticket under DT C; flag it if the VRN or an EVU rejects.
6. **Assignment to the NGO**: the standard text allows assignment with proof. Guarantees mostly pay the ticket holder (S-Bahn München: holder only; NVV: cash at a counter). Check whether the NRW, VVS, VRN, KVV and ÜSTRA forms accept a different account holder.
7. **Eigenanteil school, Azubi and Semester tickets**: is the cap basis the Eigenanteil? Only HVV (4,50 € per semester) and SH (Schulkostenträger only) regulate this.
8. **KVV**: the flyer (10/2025) and the older BB extract differ (2,50 € vs 1,50 €; 120 € vs 80 €; D-Ticket included vs not). Fetch the current KVV Beförderungsbedingungen §19.
9. **RNN, VRM guarantees, bodo, DING, HNV, naldo, VPE and the Bavarian EVU guarantees** (agilis, go-ahead, Arverio Bayern, Länderbahn/alex): not researched individually.
10. **Rounding**: DT and the standard round up to 5 ct, and MVV and VBB say so explicitly. DB's own BB round commercially, and HVV, RMV, VRR and others say nothing. Model it per tariff: default to rounding up where the standard applies.
